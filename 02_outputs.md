# UGC Ad Content Pack: Sweet Crumbs Home Bakery, Salem

*Brand type: local business. Tone: warm, friendly, real.*
*Note: Business is a sample. Scripts are templates for a real customer to say in their own words.*

---
## 1. Hooks
1. **Problem:** Birthday in two days. No cake. Now what?
2. **Curiosity:** Wait, this cake is eggless?
3. **POV:** POV: you're the friend who ordered the cake early.
4. **Question:** Ever cut a pretty cake that tasted like nothing?
5. **Budget:** Planning a birthday cake budget? Start by asking for a quote.
6. **Tip:** Tip: message your baker before the party week, not during.

---
## 2. Ad Scripts (about 30 seconds each)

### Script A (Hook 1)
- **Hook (0-3s):** "Birthday in two days. No cake. Now what?"
- **Problem (3-10s):** "Okay, I totally forgot. And I didn't want a boring shop cake. I wanted her name on it."
- **Solution (10-22s):** "So I messaged Sweet Crumbs, a home bakery in Salem. I told them my date, flavour and theme, and they told me what was possible. They do eggless too."
- **CTA (22-30s):** "DM Sweet Crumbs with your date."
- **On-screen text:** "No cake yet?" -> "Home bakery, Salem" -> "DM your date"

### Script B (Hook 2)
- **Hook (0-3s):** "Wait, this cake is eggless?"
- **Problem (3-10s):** "My family has a mix of egg and no-egg eaters. I always worry eggless means dry."
- **Solution (10-22s):** "So this time I ordered an eggless cake from Sweet Crumbs, so everyone at the table could have a slice."
- **CTA (22-30s):** "Ask Sweet Crumbs about eggless cakes."
- **On-screen text:** "Eggless?" -> "Home bakery, Salem" -> "Ask about eggless"
- *Creator note: A real customer should add their honest opinion of the taste after trying the cake.*

### Script C (Hook 6)
- **Hook (0-3s):** "Tip: message your baker before the party week."
- **Problem (3-10s):** "Last time I ordered late and stressed over every little thing."
- **Solution (10-22s):** "Now I send flavour, size, date and budget in one message to Sweet Crumbs. It's so much easier."
- **CTA (22-30s):** "Save this, then DM Sweet Crumbs your details."
- **On-screen text:** "Order early" -> "Flavour + size + date + budget" -> "DM Sweet Crumbs"

## 3. Script A on 3 Platforms

### Instagram Reels
- **Length:** 15-20 sec, fast cuts
- **Style:** Trendy, text-heavy, trending audio
- **Caption:** Forgot the cake again?
- **CTA:** DM us your date.

### Facebook
- **Length:** 30-40 sec
- **Style:** Slower, warm, family-friendly, with a bit more detail on eggless options
- **Caption:** Planning a family birthday? Sweet Crumbs makes custom cakes and cupcake boxes, with eggless options too.
- **CTA:** Message our page with your date.

### YouTube Shorts
- **Length:** About 30 sec
- **Style:** Clear first line, straight to the point
- **Title:** Custom Birthday Cake in Salem | Eggless Options | Sweet Crumbs
- **First line:** "Forgot the birthday cake? Here's what I did."
- **CTA:** Check the description for how to order.
